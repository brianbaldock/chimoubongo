Welcome

A town voted into existence

In 1971, nine people and a dog parked a school bus at a bend in a creek and voted that this would be a town. Nobody contradicted them. The mail still comes.

The story

The eleven-hour Vote, a founder in black with one eye painted, and nine rules written on the back of a bus schedule.

Getting here

Where it is on the map, Route 155 north, the NOT FAR sign, and what to bring. Bring a jar.

What to see

Two giant insect statues that are not speaking, an apology board eleven planks thick, and the Potato Bash.

The bus, at The Bend, parked there since 1971. Tomatoes have been coming through the roof vents for thirty years.
